                             Conclusion
• Capacitor assisting triple-winding transformer technique
  – independent control of each coupling coefficient
  – 𝑪𝒂𝒖𝒙 emphasizes the negative resistor at higher frequencies
     • expands the total bandwidth
     • suppresses the noise contribution from the second stage
     • improves the design trade-off among NF, gain and bandwidth.


• CTTF LNA achieves the lowest NF and a wide passband with
  minimum ripple
